Research

AI Safety and Model Research.

I study how AI models are tested and whether test results can be trusted. All research runs on small open-source models on my own laptop.

Paper · submitted · under review

A pre-flight screen for shutdown-resistance evaluations

I built a three-part check to run before a shutdown test. No model of the seven passed, so their results could not be trusted. The paper is under double-blind review at a peer-reviewed machine learning conference, with a decision in December 2026.

Paper · draft

Manipulation-Check Reporting in LLM Shutdown-Resistance Evaluations: A Systematic Review

A draft review of 19 published studies found none checked whether the model understood the scenario. The sorting was done by AI models and has not yet been checked by a person, and the draft is not submitted.

Pilot · local

JudgeCheck

JudgeCheck tests if models used as judges are biased by answer order, length or labels. On the length test, no model picked the shorter answer in both orders in a small local pilot.

Pilot · local

RU-EN Drift

I compare how small AI models perform on the same maths problems in Russian and English. In the October rerun, one model looked much stronger in English, but only 6 of its 25 apparent wins held up after three checks. Another model showed no real gap.
